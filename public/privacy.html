Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

We'd rather tell you, in plain English, what happens to your details. This page is that explanation. It meets the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

It covers santasaysmyname.com, an order for a personalised video, and the free “Santa's on his way” email form. In this policy, “we” means Neil Carroll, a UK sole trader, trading as Santa Says My Name.

In short

  • We collect your name and email, and a few details you type about the child, so we can make one video.
  • Stripe handles the card payment. We never see the card number.
  • We use a child's details only to make that video. Not for ads, and not to train AI.
  • We delete those details, and the video files, about 90 days after we send the video.
  • You can email us to see, correct or delete your information, and you can complain to the ICO.
  • We run ads on Facebook and Instagram. The Meta pixel loads only if you accept. We never send your child's details to Meta.

What we collect

About you, the person ordering

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • What you ordered, including a sibling extra or named sleigh, and whether rush delivery was included
  • An order reference, so we can match your payment to your form

You do not have to order. If you do, we need a name and an email so we can make the video and send it. The contract is with you, the adult.

About the child

Only what you type on the order form, after you have paid. It never goes on the card page.

  • First name, and how to say it
  • Age, or another detail you choose to give so the film feels personal
  • One good deed
  • A wish, if you share one
  • A short message, who the video is from, and a town or country if you want that said

We do not ask for a photo, a surname, a school, or a home address. If you leave something blank, we make the video without it. We do need a first name.

Payment

Payments go through Stripe Payment Links. Stripe collects the card details on Stripe's own page. We never see the full card number. We do see that the payment succeeded, the amount, and the email Stripe gives us for the receipt.

The free video

The “Santa's on his way” form asks for an email address only. There are no child details. It may include an optional tick if you would like occasional emails from us. We do not tick that box for you. Ordering a video does not add you to a marketing list.

Technical logs

This site is hosted on Railway. Like most hosts, Railway may keep a short log of your IP address, browser type, and the page you requested, so the site can run and stay secure. We do not use those logs to build a profile of you, and we do not use them for marketing.

Why we use it

UK GDPR says we need a lawful basis for each use. Here they are.

  • Make and deliver your video

    We use your name and email, and the child's first name, pronunciation, age or other detail, good deed and wish, to write the script, make the film, and email it to you. Order messages, such as a question about pronunciation or the download link, are part of that.

    Lawful basis: contract

  • Take payment

    Stripe Payment Links take the card payment and give us an order reference so the form and the payment belong together. Card data is handled by Stripe.

    Lawful basis: contract

  • Keep a sales record for tax

    We keep your name, email, the amount and the date, because UK tax rules require a record of the sale. This record does not include the child's details or the video.

    Lawful basis: legal obligation

  • Optional emails about future videos

    Only if you tick the optional box on the free-video form. You can unsubscribe in any marketing email, or by writing to us. Withdrawing consent does not affect the video you already ordered.

    Lawful basis: consent

  • Keep the website secure

    Short server logs help us see that the site is up and deal with faults or misuse. Our interest is a small shop that stays online and safe. The logs are brief, they are not used to market to you, and you can object.

    Lawful basis: legitimate interests

  • Measure our Meta ads

    We advertise on Facebook and Instagram. If you accept, a Meta pixel notes that you visited a page, viewed this offer, started checkout, or paid, so we can tell whether an ad worked. The pixel does not load until you accept. We do not send Meta your child's name, the good deed, the wish, or the video. If you have accepted, and we know what you paid, the purchase event includes that amount in pounds.

    Lawful basis: consent

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your child's details to train AI models. We do not make a solely automated decision that has a legal or similar effect on you or your child. A person checks every video before it is sent. That check is about quality.

Children's details

This website is for adults. We do not expect a child to place an order or fill in a form.

A parent or carer shares a few details so Santa can say the child's name and mention a good deed. Someone else, such as a grandparent, may order only if the parent is happy for those details to be shared. The order form asks them to confirm that.

We keep a child's information small, and we use it only as far as we need to make that one video.

  • We do not use it for advertising
  • We do not sell it
  • We do not use it to train AI
  • We do not build a profile of the child
  • We do not ask for photos

The voice and picture are generated from the words you gave us. If you think a child's details reached us without a parent or guardian agreeing, email us and we will delete them.

How long we keep it

  • The details you gave us to make the video, and the video files themselves, are deleted about 90 days after we email you the video. Please download it if you want to keep a copy. After we delete ours, we cannot send that file again.
  • When we delete those details, we delete the order-form copy too, and we ask the voice and video tools to delete the script they held for us.
  • We keep a basic sales record — your name, email, what you paid and when — for 6 years, because UK tax law asks us to. It does not include the child's name, good deed, wish, or the video.
  • Stripe keeps payment records under its own legal duties. We never hold the card number, so we cannot delete Stripe's copy of it.
  • If you opted into occasional emails, we keep that email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.
  • Server logs are kept for a short time, then deleted.

If you ask us to delete the video details sooner, we will, unless we still need the basic sales record for tax.

Cookies

This website does not set its own cookies. The page where your finished video plays has no tracking, and the Meta pixel is not on that page.

We run Meta ads. The Meta pixel sets cookies to measure those ads, and only after you accept. A banner offers Accept and No thanks. If you choose No thanks, the pixel stays off. UK cookie rules (the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations) say we must ask before we set a cookie that is not needed for the site to work. Your choice is remembered in this browser. To choose again, clear the site data for santasaysmyname.com and reload the page.

If you go on to pay, you are on Stripe's website, and Stripe uses its own cookies there. The order form and the free-video form are on Tally, which may use cookies on Tally's pages. Their policies explain those.

We do not need a strictly necessary cookie to run this site, and we do not set one.

We also keep a count of how many times the pre-order page is opened, grouped only by the campaign names in the link (utm_source, utm_campaign and utm_content). That count is a total. We do not store your IP address for it, and we do not set a cookie for it.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to:

  • Tell you whether we hold personal information about you, and for a copy of it
  • Correct anything that is wrong
  • Delete it
  • Limit how we use it
  • Give you a copy of information you provided, in a common format, where we rely on contract or consent
  • Stop a use that is based on legitimate interests. We will stop unless we have a strong reason that overrides your objection
  • Stop marketing emails. We will stop

Where we rely on consent, you can take it back at any time. That does not undo something we already did while we had it.

These rights are yours. For a child's information, a parent or guardian can use them on the child's behalf.

Email hello@santasaysmyname.com. We will reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm it is you. We do not charge for a normal request. If a request is excessive, we will tell you before we do anything else.
