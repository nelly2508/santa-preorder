Losing the 14-day cancellation right does not leave you stuck with a video we got wrong, or with no video at all. The Nice List Promise sits on top of your legal rights.

  • Our mistake with the name. If we do not follow the name or the pronunciation you gave us, we remake the video free of charge. We aim to send the remake within 24 hours of you telling us.
  • Still not right. If you are still unhappy after that, email us within 7 days of delivery and we will refund what you paid.
  • We cannot deliver. If a standard order is not with you by the date stated on the order page, or a rush order is not with you within 48 hours, we refund you in full. If the video turns up late and you still want it, you can keep it.
  • We cannot make it at all. Full refund.
  • You change your mind before we have started. Full refund.

The Consumer Rights Act 2015 still applies. Digital content must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose, and as described. If it is not — for example the file will not play, or it is not the video we described — you can ask for a remake or a refund, as that Act allows. Nothing in these terms takes those rights away.

To ask for a remake or a refund, reply to your order email or write to hello@santasaysmyname.com. We refund to the original card, through Stripe, as soon as we reasonably can and within 14 days of agreeing the refund.
