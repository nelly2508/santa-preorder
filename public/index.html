FAQ

Questions

How is it made?

Santa is an original AI character with an original AI voice. Not an actor, and not a copy of anyone. We add your child's details to his script, make the voice, and lip-sync it to our fireside film. A real person watches every video before it's sent. Videos are AI-generated.

Will he say the name right?

Yes. You tell us how it's said (“Saoirse = SEER-sha”). If it's still wrong, we remake it free within 24 hours.

Do you need a photo of my child?

No. No photos, no surnames, no school. Just a first name and a few details.

Adding a brother or sister?

Adding a brother or sister? After payment, the order form asks for each child's name, how to say it and their good deed. You can add up to 4 siblings, on any checkout.

What happens to my child's details?

We use them only to make the video. They're never used for ads or to train AI. We collect them on the order form after payment, never on the card page. We delete your child's details and the video files about 90 days after we send the video, so download your video to keep it. The full notice is in our privacy policy.

Can grandparents order?

Yes. You tick a box to confirm the parent is happy for you to share the details. We send the video to you to pass on.

What if I'm not happy?

The Nice List Promise: we remake it, then refund you. Wrong name: free remake within 24 hours. Still not right: full refund within 7 days of delivery.

Will Santa promise a present?

Only if you tell us it's definitely under the tree. Otherwise he'll say he'll “see what the elves can do”.

Can I share it?

Yes. It's yours. Play it on the TV, or send it to family.

When does it arrive?

Pay now. Order by Sat 12 Dec and it arrives by Tue 15 Dec. Pre-orders go out in batches from 1 Dec. From 13 to 18 Dec it's rush only, within 48 hours.

Privacy

Your child's details

We collect a child's details only on the order form, after you've paid. They never go into the payment. We delete the details used to make the video, and the video files, about 90 days after we send it.

This is the short version. The privacy policy has the full detail, including cookies, how long we keep things, and how to complain to the ICO. The terms of sale are the contract for your order.

Who we are

Neil Carroll trading as Santa Says My Name, hello@santasaysmyname.com. We are the data controller. The postal address is on the privacy policy.

What we collect

From you, the buyer: your name, email and order details. Stripe handles card payments; we never see your card. About your child, only what you type on the order form: first name and how to say it, age or another detail you choose to give, one good deed, one wish, a short personal message, and who it's from and where they live (town or country only). We never ask for photos, surnames, schools or addresses.

Why

To make and deliver the video you've ordered. Lawful basis: contract with you. We also keep a basic sales record because tax law says so, and we only send optional news if you tick the box on the free-video form. The privacy policy lists every purpose and lawful basis.

Who helps us

Stripe (payments), Tally (order form), Railway (this website), ElevenLabs and other AI voice and video tools, our email provider and our file host. They act for us under contract. Some are outside the UK, under the safeguards in the privacy policy. Meta is included only if we run ads later, and only with your consent.

What we don't do

No ads from your child's details, no selling, no AI training on those details, and no tracking on the page where the video plays.

How long

Your child's details and the video files are deleted about 90 days after delivery. Download your video to keep it. We keep a basic order record (your name, email, amount) for 6 years for tax.

Your rights

Ask to see, correct or delete your data, or to use your other rights, by emailing hello@santasaysmyname.com. You can complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk. The privacy policy sets the rights out in full.
